    Why Waiver

    86% of the time, Waiver put two players in the right order when their results were clearly different. The average of expert rankings managed 83%, and going by recent points alone, 80%.

    • An AI model trained on every play Five seasons of NFL play-by-play teach it what actually leads to points.
    • Usage, not last week's box score Targets, carries and snaps predict future points better than points already scored.
    • Lucky touchdowns don't fool it A player scoring more than his chances support is marked down, not chased.
    • It knows who is playing Depth charts and injury reports push backups and injured players down.

    Tested on every week of the 2024 and 2025 seasons, predicting four weeks ahead, and graded in public every week on the scorecard. Past accuracy is no guarantee of future results.
